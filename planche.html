Skill page

Planche

Goal

What it is

The planche is a straight-body hold where the shoulders stay active and the body stays horizontal to the floor. It demands full-body tension and serious shoulder strength.

Hardest
1

Starting point

Start with shoulder taps, wall planches, and bent-arm support holds to build tension and balance.

Tension
2

Progression

Move into elevated feet planches, tucked planches, and then longer straight-body holds as your shoulders and core improve.

Control
3

Advanced

Hold a full straight planche with a strong scapular position, active core, and clean shoulder alignment.
