Do I need equipment?

No. Most routines use your bodyweight, a pull-up bar, and optional parallel bars for extra progression.

How long before I see results?

Most people notice better energy, posture, and confidence within 4–6 weeks when they train consistently.

Is this good for beginners?

Yes. The program begins with skill foundations, mobility work, and simple progressions before harder variations.

Can I train at home?

Absolutely. The plan is designed to work at home, in a park, or in a personal training environment.
