What is calisthenics?

Calisthenics is beautiful strength.

Calisthenics is a form of bodyweight training that uses your own body as resistance to build strength, mobility, balance, coordination, and endurance. The Greek roots kalos and sthenos mean “beautiful strength.”

Skill focus

Calisthenics is built around skill and control.

Calisthenics is the practice of building strength, balance, and body control through skill-based movements using only the body as resistance. Every progression teaches positioning, tension, and control.

The name comes from the Greek kalos and sthenos, meaning “beautiful strength”. In practice, that means developing powerful, elegant movement through disciplined skill progressions.

Skill progression

How the ladder works.

01

Foundation

Start with control, tension, and clean movement patterns before chasing harder holds.

  • Push-ups and body tension
  • Pull-up basics
  • Core strength and balance
02

Intermediate

Build strength through harder progressions that demand more shoulder stability and body control.

  • Dip and pull-up mastery
  • Handstand preparation
  • Front lever and flag basics
03

Advanced

Elite skills come from long-term tension, positioning, and consistency rather than rushing the ladder.

  • Planche and lever work
  • Handstand push-up variations
  • Muscle-up and advanced control
