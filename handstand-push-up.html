Handstand Push Up

Goal

What it is

A handstand push-up is an overhead press done upside down. It develops balance, shoulder strength, trunk stiffness, and control under load.

Advanced
1

Build the base

Work on wall handstands, shoulder mobility, and strictly controlled pike push-ups to build the right pattern.

Balance
2

Progression

Move into pike push-ups, half handstand push-ups, and then assisted reps before working toward full range reps.

Pressing
3

Advanced control

Clean reps require stable shoulders, long lines, and repeated tension from the floor up to the top position.
