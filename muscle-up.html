Muscle Up

Goal

What it is

A muscle-up combines a pull-up and a dip into one continuous movement. It is powerful, explosive, and a classic expression of bodyweight strength.

Advanced
1

Build the base

Strengthen pull-ups, dips, and the transition through controlled reps and scapular movement.

Pull
2

Progression

Use false grip pull-ups, negative muscle-ups, and transition drills to connect the pull with the dip.

Transition
3

Mastery

Clean muscle-ups come from strong timing, controlled shoulders, and smooth body lines over the bar.
